I have conducted experiments on fine-tuning and evaluating open-source large language models. This work includes parameter-efficient fine-tuning, model capability analysis, and experimental design for studying LLM behavior.

Technologies explored include PyTorch, Qwen models, LoRA, and reinforcement learning.
